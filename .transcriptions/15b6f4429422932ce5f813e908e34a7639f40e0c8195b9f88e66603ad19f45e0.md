**Proposition**

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} (-1)^{n+1} b_n$ be an alternating series satisfying the conditions of the alternating test and let $S$ be the sum of the series. Then, $S$ lies between any two consecutive partial sums $S_n$ and $S_{n+1}$. Moreover, for every $n$, we have

$$|R_n| = |S - S_n| \leq b_{n+1}$$
