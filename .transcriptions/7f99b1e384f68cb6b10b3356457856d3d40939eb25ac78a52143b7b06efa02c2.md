The case n=2

**Corollary**
For any two rvs $X_1$ and $X_2$, and any constants $a_1, a_2, b$,

$$E(a_1X_1 + a_2X_2 + b) = a_1E(X_1) + a_2E(X_2) + b$$

and

$$\text{Var}(a_1X_1 + a_2X_2 + b) = a_1^2\text{Var}(X_1) + a_2^2\text{Var}(X_2) + 2a_1a_2\text{Cov}(X_1, X_2)$$

In particular, $E(X_1 + X_2) = E(X_1) + E(X_2)$ and, if $X_1$ and $X_2$ are independent, $\text{Var}(X_1 + X_2) = \text{Var}(X_1) + \text{Var}(X_2)$.¹ Also, $E(X_1 - X_2) = E(X_1) - E(X_2)$ and, if $X_1$ and $X_2$ are independent,

$$\text{Var}(X_1 - X_2) = \text{Var}(X_1) + \text{Var}(X_2).$$
