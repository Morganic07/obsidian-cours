Theorem: If $X$ is the number of successes in a random sample of size $n$ drawn from a population consisting of $M$ successes and $(N - M)$ failures, then the probability distribution of $X$, called the hypergeometric distribution, is given by

$$P(X = x) = h(x; n, M, N) = \frac{\dbinom{M}{x}\dbinom{N-M}{n-x}}{\dbinom{N}{n}}$$
