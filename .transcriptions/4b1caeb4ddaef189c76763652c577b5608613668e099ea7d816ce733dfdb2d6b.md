With sequences, we obtain the following definition:

> **Definition**
>
> Let $(u_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ and $(v_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be two real sequences such that $v_n$ does not vanish beyond some index. We say that $(u_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is a little $o$ of $(v_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ if
>
> $$\lim_{n\to+\infty} \frac{u_n}{v_n} = 0$$
>
> In this case, we write $u_n \underset{+\infty}{=} o(v_n)$.
