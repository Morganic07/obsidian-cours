**Proposition**

Let $n_0 \in \mathbb{N}$ and $f$ be a continuous positive decreasing function on $[n_0, +\infty[$. For $n \geq n_0$, let $a_n = f(n)$. Suppose that the series $\sum_{n \geq n_0} a_n$ is convergent and let $S$ be its sum. Then, for every $n \geq n_0$, we have:

$$S_n + \int_{n+1}^{+\infty} f(x)\,dx \leq S \leq S_n + \int_{n}^{+\infty} f(x)\,dx$$
