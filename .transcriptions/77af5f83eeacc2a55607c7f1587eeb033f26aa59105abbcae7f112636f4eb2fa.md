❶ For $x \in [-1,1]$:

$$\sqrt{1+x} = 1 + \frac{x}{2} + \sum_{n=2}^{+\infty} (-1)^{n+1} \frac{1\cdot 3\cdot 5 \ldots (2n-3)}{n!\,2^n} x^n$$

$$= \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} (-1)^{n+1} \frac{\binom{2n}{n}}{(2n-1)2^{2n}} x^n$$

❷ For $x \in ]-1,1]$:

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x}} = 1 + \sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} (-1)^{n} \frac{1\cdot 3\cdot 5 \ldots (2n-1)}{n!\,2^n} x^n$$

$$= \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} (-1)^{n} \frac{\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{2n}} x^n$$
