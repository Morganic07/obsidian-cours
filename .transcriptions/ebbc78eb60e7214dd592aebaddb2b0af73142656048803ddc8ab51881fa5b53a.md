**Theorem**

If $f$ is a periodic function with period $2\pi$ such that $f$ and $f'$ are piece-wise continuous on $[-\pi, \pi]$, then the Fourier series of $f$ is pointwise convergent. The sum of this Fourier series is $f(x)$ at all $x$ where $f$ is continuous. At a real number $x$ where $f$ is discontinuous, the sum if the Fourier series is the average of the right and left limits, that is

$$\frac{1}{2}\left(f(x^+) + f(x^-)\right)$$

where

$$f(x^+) = \lim_{t \to x^+} f(t) \text{ and } f(x^-) = \lim_{t \to x^-} f(t)$$
