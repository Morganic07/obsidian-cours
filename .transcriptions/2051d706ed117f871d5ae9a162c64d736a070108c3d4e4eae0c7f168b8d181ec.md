**Theorem**

Let $a < b$ be two real numbers, $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ a sequence of functions defined on the interval $[a, b]$ and a function $f : [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$. Assume that

1. $\forall n \in \mathbb{N}$, $f_n$ is continuous on $[a, b]$.
2. The sequence $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ converges uniformly to $f$ on $[a, b]$.

Then $f$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ and the sequence $\left(\int_a^b f_n(t)\,dt\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ converges to $\int_a^b f(t)\,dt$, that is

$$\lim_{n\to+\infty} \int_a^b f_n(t)\,dt = \int_a^b \lim_{n\to+\infty} f_n(t)\,dt$$
