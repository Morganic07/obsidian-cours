**Definition**

Let $(a_n)_{\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of real numbers.

1. We say that the sequence converges to $+\infty$ if

$$\forall A \in \mathbb{R}^+, \exists N \in \mathbb{N}, \forall n \geq N, \ a_n \geq A$$

2. We say that the sequence converges to $-\infty$ if

$$\forall B \in \mathbb{R}^+, \exists N \in \mathbb{N}, \forall n \geq N, \ a_n \leq -B$$

Study the sequences defined by $a_n = \frac{n^2}{\sqrt{n^3+4n}}$ for $n \geq 1$ and $a_n = -n^2 + 2n - 4$ for $n \geq 0$.
