**Definition**

Let $a$ and $r$ be a nonzero real numbers. The series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} ar^n$ is called a geometric series with ratio $r$.

**Theorem**

Let $a$ and $r$ be a nonzero real numbers. The geometric series series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} ar^n$ is is convergent if, and only if, $|r| < 1$. In that case, its sum is given by $S = \frac{a}{1-r}$.
