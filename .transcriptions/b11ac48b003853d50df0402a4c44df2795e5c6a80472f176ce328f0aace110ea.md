Theorem

Let $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of functions defined on a set $X \subset \mathbb{R}$ and $a$ be a limit point of $X$. Assume that

- $\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\ \exists \ell_n \in \mathbb{R},\ \lim_{x\to a} f_n(x) = \ell_n$
- The series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ converges uniformly on $X$ to a function $S : X \to \mathbb{R}$

Then the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} \ell_n$ is convergent and $\lim_{x\to a} S(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} \ell_n$. That is

$$\lim_{x\to a}\left(\sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} f_n(x)\right) = \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} \lim_{x\to a} f_n(x)$$
