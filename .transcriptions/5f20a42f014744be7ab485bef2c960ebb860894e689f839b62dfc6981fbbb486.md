- The two **blue** lines (see Figure) are **geometrically perpendicular if and only if**: "the distance from $\mathbf{u}$ to $\mathbf{v}$ is the same as the distance from $\mathbf{u}$ to $-\mathbf{v}$".
- This is the same as requiring the squares of the distances to be the same.

Figure : deux droites bleues se coupent en $\mathbf{0}$, l'une passant par $\mathbf{u}$, l'autre par $\mathbf{v}$ et $-\mathbf{v}$. Les distances sont notées $\|\mathbf{u} - \mathbf{v}\|$ et $\|\mathbf{u} - (-\mathbf{v})\|$.
