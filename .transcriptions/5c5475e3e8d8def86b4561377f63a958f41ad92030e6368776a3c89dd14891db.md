The **dimension** of a nonzero subspace $H$, denoted by $\dim H$, is the number of vectors in any basis for $H$. The dimension of the zero subspace $\{\mathbf{0}\}$ is defined to be zero.²

² The zero subspace has *no* basis (because the zero vector by itself forms a linearly dependent set)

The space $\mathbb{R}^n$ has dimension $n$. Every basis for $\mathbb{R}^n$ consists of $n$ vectors. A plane through $\mathbf{0}$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$ is two-dimensional, and a line through $\mathbf{0}$ is one-dimensional.
