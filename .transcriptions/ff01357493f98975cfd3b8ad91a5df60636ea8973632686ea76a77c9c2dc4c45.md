Any factorization $A = U\Sigma V^T$, with $U$ and $V$ orthogonal, $\Sigma$ as in (3), and positive diagonal entries in $D$, is called a **singular value decomposition** (or **SVD**) of $A$. The matrices $U$ and $V$ are not uniquely determined by $A$, but the diagonal entries of $\Sigma$ are necessarily the singular values of $A$.

- The columns of $U$ are called **left singular vectors** of $A$
- The columns of $V$ are called **right singular vectors** of $A$
