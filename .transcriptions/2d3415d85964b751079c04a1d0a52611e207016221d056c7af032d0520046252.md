- If the series is of the form $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}}(-1)^n b_n$ or $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}}(-1)^{n+1} b_n$, then the alternating series test is an obvious possibility.
- When series involve factorials or products, the ratio test may apply, except for $p$-series for which the limit of $\left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right|$ is 1.
- If $a_n$ is of the form $(b_n)^n$, then the root test may be useful.
- If $a_n = f(n)$ where $\int_1^{+\infty} f(x)\,dx$ is easily evaluated and $f$ satisfies the right conditions, then the integral test applies.
