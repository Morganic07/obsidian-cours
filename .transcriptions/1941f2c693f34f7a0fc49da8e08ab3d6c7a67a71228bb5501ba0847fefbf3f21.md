By comparing the area of the shaded rectangles on the first figure with the area under the curve $y = f(x)$ from 1 to $n$, we obtain:

$$S_n - a_1 = a_2 + \ldots + a_n \leq \int_1^n f(x)\,dx \quad (1)$$

Similarly, using the second figure, we get:

$$\int_1^n f(x)\,dx \leq a_1 + a_2 + \ldots + a_{n-1} = S_{n-1} \quad (2)$$
