**Confidence Interval on the Variance**

If $s^2$ is the sample variance from a random sample of $n$ observations from a normal distribution with unknown variance $\sigma^2$, then a **100(1 − α)% confidence interval on $\sigma^2$ is**

$$\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_{\alpha/2,n-1}} \le \sigma^2 \le \frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_{1-\alpha/2,n-1}} \qquad (1)$$

where $\chi^2_{\alpha/2,n-1}$ and $\chi^2_{1-\alpha/2,n-1}$ are the upper and lower $100\alpha/2$ percentage points of the chi-square distribution with $n-1$ degrees of freedom, respectively. A **confidence interval for $\sigma$** has lower and upper limits that are the square roots of the corresponding limits in Equation (1)
