We have the following examples with $\lim_{n\to+\infty}\left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right| = 1$ and different results on the convergence or divergence of the series

1. If $a_n = (-1)^n \frac{n}{n^2+1}$, then the series is conditionally convergent.
2. If $a_n = \frac{1}{n}$, then the series is divergent.
3. If $a_n = (-1)^n \frac{1}{n^2+1}$, the series is absolutely convergent.
