An undirected graph is a triplet $G = (V, E, \gamma)$ where:

- $V$ is a non-empty finite set: each element of $V$ is called a vertex of $G$.
- $E$ is a finite (possibly empty) set: each element of $E$ is called an edge of $G$.
- $\gamma$ is a function from $E$ to $V^2$.

For every $e \in E$, the components of $\gamma(e)$ are called the endpoints of $e$.
