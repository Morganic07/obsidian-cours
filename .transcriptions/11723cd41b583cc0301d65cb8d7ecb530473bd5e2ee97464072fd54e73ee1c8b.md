Suppose $\{\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, \mathbf{v}_n\}$ is an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{R}^n$ consisting of eigenvectors of $A^TA$, arranged so that the corresponding eigenvalues of $A^TA$ satisfy $\lambda_1 \ge \cdots \ge \lambda_n$, and suppose $A$ has $r$ nonzero singular values. Then $\{A\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, A\mathbf{v}_r\}$ is an orthogonal basis for Col $A$, and rank $A = r$.

(rank $A$ = the dimension of the column space of $A$)
