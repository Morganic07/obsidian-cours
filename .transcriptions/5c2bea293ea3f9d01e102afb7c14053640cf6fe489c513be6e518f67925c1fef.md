- Testing of hypothesis is not decision making itself; but it helps for decision making.

- Tests do not explain the reasons as why the difference exist, they only indicate that the difference is e.g. due to fluctuations of sampling or because of other reasons.

- Tests are based on probabilities and as such they cannot be expressed/interpreted with full certainty.

- Statistical inferences based on significance tests cannot be said to be entirely correct evidences concerning the truth of the hypothesis.
