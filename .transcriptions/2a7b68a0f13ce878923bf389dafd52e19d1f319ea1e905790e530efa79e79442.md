**DEFINITION** If after observing $X_1 = x_1, X_2 = x_2, \ldots, X_n = x_n$, we compute the observed sample mean $\bar{x}$ and then substitute $\bar{x}$ into (B) in place of $\overline{X}$, the resulting fixed interval is called a **95% confidence interval for $\mu$**. This CI can be expressed either as

$\left(\bar{x} - 1.96 \cdot \dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}, \bar{x} + 1.96 \cdot \dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}\right)$ is a 95% confidence interval for $\mu$

or as

$\bar{x} - 1.96 \cdot \dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}} < \mu < \bar{x} + 1.96 \cdot \dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$ with 95% confidence
