**t-test (Student's t-test): Example 1a**

A consulting firm claims that its consultants earn on **average** **exactly** 260€ an hour.

- You decide to test the claim using a sample of 16 consultants.
- You find that $\bar{X}$= 200€ and s = 96€.
- Test the claim at $\alpha = 0.05$ level.
- Assume that the population follows a normal distribution.

We follow the same steps in hypothesis testing described in previous lectures.

➢ **Step 1**: *Formulate the null and alternate hypotheses*

H$_0$: $\mu = 260$€

H$_1$: $\mu \neq 260$€
