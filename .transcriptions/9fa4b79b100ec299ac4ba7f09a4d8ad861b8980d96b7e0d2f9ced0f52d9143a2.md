**DEFINITION** A nonnegative rv $X$ is said to have a **lognormal distribution** if the rv $Y = \ln(X)$ has a normal distribution. The resulting pdf of a lognormal rv when $\ln(X)$ is normally distributed with parameters $\mu$ and $\sigma$ is

$$f(x; \mu, \sigma) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sigma x} e^{-[\ln(x)-\mu]^2/(2\sigma^2)} \qquad x > 0$$
