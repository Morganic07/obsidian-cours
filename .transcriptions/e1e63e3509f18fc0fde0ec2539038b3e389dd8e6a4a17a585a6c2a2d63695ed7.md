Corollary

Let $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of functions defined on a set $X \subset \mathbb{R}$. If the the sequence of functions $f_n = S_n - S_{n-1}$ does not converges uniformly to the zero function on $X$, then the series of functions $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ does not converges uniformly on $X$.

**Proof.**
We use the contrapositive of the previous proposition.
