This comes from the equality $R_n = S - S_n$ where $S$ is the sum of the convergent series and $S_n$ is the $n$th partial sum of the series.

> **Remark**
>
> If $\sum_{k \geq n+1} a_n$ has no limit or does not tend to 0 when $n$ tends to $+\infty$, then the series $\sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} a_n$ is divergent.
