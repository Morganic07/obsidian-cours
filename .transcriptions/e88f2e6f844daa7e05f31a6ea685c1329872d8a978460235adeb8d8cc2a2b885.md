$\begin{aligned} 2x + 3y &= 5 \\ 4x + 2y &= 6 \end{aligned}$ or $\left[\begin{array}{cc|c} 2 & 3 & 5 \\ 4 & 2 & 6 \end{array}\right]$

**Row picture** : plot both equations. Answer is intersection between two lines (in 3D, answer is point intersection of three planes).

**Column picture** : $x\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix} + y\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ 6 \end{bmatrix}$. Plot vectors of the columns of $A$. Answer is the "amount" of each vector to reach the answer : 1 of vector 1 and one of vector 2 = (5, 6).

Figures : à gauche, les deux droites $2x + 3y = 5$ et $4x + 2y = 6$ qui se croisent ; à droite, les vecteurs $(2, 4)$ et $(3, 2)$ dont la somme donne $(5, 6)$.
