**DEFINITION** A $100(1-\alpha)\%$ **confidence interval** for the mean $\mu$ of a normal population when the value of $\sigma$ is known is given by

$$\left(\overline{x} - z_{\alpha/2} \cdot \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}},\ \overline{x} + z_{\alpha/2} \cdot \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}\right)$$

or, equivalently, by $\overline{x} \pm z_{\alpha/2} \cdot \sigma/\sqrt{n}$.
