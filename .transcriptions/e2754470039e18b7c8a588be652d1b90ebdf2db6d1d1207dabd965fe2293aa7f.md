$$y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X_1 + \beta_2 X_2 + \dots + \beta_k X_k + \varepsilon$$

where $k$ is the number of variables, or parameters.
The parameters in the linear regression model are very easy to interpret.

- $\beta_0$ is the intercept (i.e. the average value for y if all the $X$'s are zero),

- $\beta_j$ is the slope for the $j$-th variable $X_j$

- $\beta_j$ is the average increase in y when $X_j$ is increased by one and
  **all other *X*'s are held constant**.
