LAW OF LARGE NUMBERS

If $X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n$ is a random sample from a distribution with mean $\mu$ and finite variance, then $\bar{X}$ converges to $\mu$

1. In mean square: $E\left[(\bar{X} - \mu)^2\right] \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$
2. In probability: $P(|\bar{X} - \mu| \geq \varepsilon) \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$ for any $\varepsilon > 0$
