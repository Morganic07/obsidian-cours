Let $X$ be the no. of beds occupied in a hospital's ER at a certain time of day. Suppose the pmf of $X$ is given by

| $x$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $p(x)$ | .20 | .25 | .30 | .15 | .10 |

Then the probability that at most two beds are occupied is

$P(X \leq 2) = p(0) + p(1) + p(2) = 0.75$

Note also that $\{X \leq 2.7\}$ and $\{X \leq 2\}$ are the same events because $X$ has discrete values, so $P(X \leq 2.7) = P(X \leq 2) = 0.75$. Similarly $P(X \leq 2.99) = 0.75$
