**Corollary**

Let $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of functions defined on a set $X \subset \mathbb{R}$. Assume that

- Each function $f_n$ is continuous on $X$,
- The series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ converges uniformly on $X$.

Then the function $S = \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} f_n$ is continuous on $X$.
