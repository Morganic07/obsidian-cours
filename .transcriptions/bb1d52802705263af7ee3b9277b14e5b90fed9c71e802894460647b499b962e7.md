Proposition

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ and $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} b_n$ be two series with positive terms sucht that $\lim_{n\to+\infty} \frac{a_n}{b_n} = c$ with $c$ a finite strictly positive real number. Then the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ converges if, and only if, the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} b_n$ converges.
