**Comparison of Pearson and Spearman coefficients**

1. The fundamental difference between the two correlation coefficients is that the **Pearson coefficient** works with a **linear relationship** between the two variables whereas the **Spearman Coefficient** works with **monotonic relationships** as well.

2. One more difference is that **Pearson works with raw data values** of the variables while **Spearman works with rank-ordered** variables.

- Now, if we feel that a scatterplot is visually indicating a "might be monotonic, might be linear" relationship, our best bet would be to apply Spearman and not Pearson.
- No harm would be done by switching to Spearman even if the data turned out to be perfectly linear.
- But, if it's not exactly linear and we use Pearson's coefficient then we'll miss out on the information that Spearman could capture.
