**PROPOSITION**

For any two random variables $X$ and $Y$,

1. $\mathrm{Cov}(X, Y) = \mathrm{Cov}(Y, X)$
2. $\mathrm{Cov}(X, X) = \mathrm{Var}(X)$
3. (Covariance shortcut formula) $\mathrm{Cov}(X, Y) = E(XY) - \mu_X \cdot \mu_Y$
4. (Distributive property of covariance) For any rv $Z$ and any constants, $a, b, c$,

$$\mathrm{Cov}(aX + bY + c, Z) = a\,\mathrm{Cov}(X, Z) + b\,\mathrm{Cov}(Y, Z)$$
