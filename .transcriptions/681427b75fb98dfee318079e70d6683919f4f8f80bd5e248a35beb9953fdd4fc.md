Theorem

If $f$ has a power series representation (expansion) at $x = \alpha$, and $f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} a_n (x-\alpha)^n$, then its coefficients are given by the formula $a_n = \frac{f^{(n)}(\alpha)}{n!}$. In other words, we have

$$\forall x \in ]\alpha - R, \alpha + R[,\ f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(\alpha)}{n!}(x-\alpha)^n$$
