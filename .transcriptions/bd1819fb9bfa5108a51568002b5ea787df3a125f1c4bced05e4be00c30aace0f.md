**Definition**

Let $(a_n)_{\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of real numbers. We say that the sequence is convergent if there exists $\ell \in \mathbb{R}$ such that the sequence converges to $\ell$. Otherwise, we say that the sequence is divergent, that is it either it tends $+\infty$, or it tend to $-\infty$, or it does not admit a limit $\ell \in \mathbb{R}$.
