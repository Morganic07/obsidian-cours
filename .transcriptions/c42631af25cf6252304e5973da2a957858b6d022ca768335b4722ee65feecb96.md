➢ **Step 2**: $\alpha = 0.05$

➢ **Step 3**: *Choose the test statistic*

We are going to use the t-distribution since $n$ is small (only 16) and we do not know σ

Figure : Courbe en cloche de la distribution $t_{15}$, avec les deux zones de rejet colorées en magenta aux extrémités, de 2.5 % chacune, délimitées par les valeurs critiques -2.1315 à gauche et 2.1315 à droite.

➢ **Step 4**: *Establish the critical value or values of the test statistic needed to reject $H_0$.*

- This is a $t_{15}$ so we **cannot** use the **critical values** of $\pm 1.96$ which we used for a $Z$ test.
- If you go to the t-table (next slide) and examine the column that has 0.025 on the top and the row for 15 degrees of freedom, you will find that the critical values are $\pm 2.1315$.
- Please do not forget that you have $(n-1)$ degrees of freedom (d.f.); $n = 16$, but d.f. = 15.
