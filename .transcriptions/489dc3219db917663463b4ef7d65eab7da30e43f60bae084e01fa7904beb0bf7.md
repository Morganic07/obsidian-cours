**Definition**

Let $a \in \overline{\mathbb{R}} = \mathbb{R} \cup \{\pm\infty\}$. A neighborhood of $a$, denoted by $\nu(a)$ is a subset of $\mathbb{R}$ containing an interval of the form:

1. $]a - \epsilon, a + \epsilon[$, where $\epsilon > 0$, if $a \in \mathbb{R}$.
2. $]A, +\infty[$ if $a = +\infty$.
3. $]-\infty, A[$ if $a = -\infty$.

We say that a property is satisfied near $a$ when it is satisfied on a neighborhood of $a$.
