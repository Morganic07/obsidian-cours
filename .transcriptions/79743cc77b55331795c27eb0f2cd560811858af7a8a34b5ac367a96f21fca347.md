**Corollary**

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ be a series with positive real terms.

1. If there exists a real number $p > 1$ such that $\lim_{n\to+\infty} n^p a_n = 0$, then the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ converges.
2. If there exists a real number $p \leq 1$ such that $\lim_{n\to+\infty} n^p a_n = +\infty$, then the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ diverges.
