Weighted Graph

A weighted undirected graph is a quadruple $G = (V, E, \gamma, w)$ where:

- $G = (V, E, \gamma)$ is an undirected graph;
- $w$ is a function from $E$ to $\mathbb{R}$ called the weighting of $G$.

Adjacency Matrix

Let $G = (V, E, \gamma, w)$ be a simple weighted undirected graph. The adjacency matrix of $G$ is a square matrix $M$ of order $n = \text{card}(V)$ such that:

$$M_{i,j} = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } i = j \\ w(v_i v_j) & \text{if } v_i v_j \in E \\ +\infty & \text{if } v_i v_j \notin E \end{cases}$$
