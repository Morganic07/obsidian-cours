**Corollary**

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ and $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} b_n$ be two series with positive terms such that

$$a_n = o(b_n)$$

1. If $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} b_n$ converges, then $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ converges.
2. If $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ diverges, then $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} b_n$ diverges.

**Proof** If $a_n = o(b_n)$, then $a_n = O(b_n)$ and we apply the proposition.
