**Definition**

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ be a convergent series with sum $S$ and let $S_n$ be the partial $n$th sum. The $n$th remainder of the series is defined by $R_n = S - S_n = \sum_{k\geq n+1} a_k$.

**Proposition**

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ be a convergent series and $R_n$ be the $n$th remainder. Then $\lim_{n\to+\infty} R_n = 0$.
