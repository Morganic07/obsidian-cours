- If all the points are on the line, $r = 1$ (or $-1$ if there is an inverse relationship), then $r^2$ is 100%.
- This means that all of the variation in Y is explained by (variations in) X.
- This indicates that **X does a perfect job in explaining Y** and **there is no unexplained variation**.
- Thus,
  - if $r = 0.30$ (or $-0.30$), then $r^2 = 0.09$. Only 9% of the variation in Y is explained by X and 91% is unexplained. This is why a correlation coefficient of 0.30 is considered **weak** - even if it is significant.
  - If $r = 0.50$ (or $-0.50$), then $r^2 = 0.25$. 25% of the variation in Y is explained by X and 75% is unexplained. This is why a correlation coefficient of 0.50 is considered **moderate**.
  - If $r = 0.90$ (or $-0.90$), then $r^2 = 0.81$. 81% of the variation in Y is explained by X and 19% is unexplained. This is why a correlation coefficient of 0.90 is considered **very strong**.
