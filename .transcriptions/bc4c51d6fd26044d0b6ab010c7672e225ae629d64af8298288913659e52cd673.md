1. Arrange the data in increasing order.

2. Divide the data at the median into two equal groups: below the median (low) and above the median (high)

3. Find the median of the low group: this is called the First Quartile: $Q_1$

4. Find the median of the high group, this is the Third Quartile: $Q_3$
(Note that the median corresponds to the second quartile)

The INTERQUARTILE RANGE (IQR) is the difference between Third and First quartiles $IQR = Q_3 - Q_1$

An OUTLIER is a point that falls outside the interval
$[Q_1 - \frac{3}{2}\text{IQR},\ Q_3 + \frac{3}{2}\text{ IQR}]$
