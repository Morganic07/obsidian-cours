The hostname serves several important purposes:

1. **Identification:** It uniquely identifies a computer or device on a network. Each device should have a distinct hostname to avoid conflicts and confusion.
2. **User-Friendly Address:** Instead of using IP addresses, which can be difficult to remember, hostnames provide a human-readable way to refer to devices on the network.
3. **Networking Functions:** Hostnames are used in various networking functions, including email addressing and accessing services over a network.
