**PROPOSITION**

Let $X$ and $Y$ be jointly distributed rvs with pmf $p(x, y)$ or pdf $f(x, y)$ according to whether the variables are discrete or continuous. Then the expected value of a function $h(X, Y)$, denoted by $E[h(X, Y)]$ or $\mu_{h(X,Y)}$, is given by

$$E[h(X,Y)] = \begin{cases} \displaystyle\sum_x \sum_y h(x,y) \cdot p(x,y) & \text{if } X \text{ and } Y \text{ are discrete} \\ \displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} h(x,y) \cdot f(x,y)\,dx\,dy & \text{if } X \text{ and } Y \text{ are continuous} \end{cases}$$
