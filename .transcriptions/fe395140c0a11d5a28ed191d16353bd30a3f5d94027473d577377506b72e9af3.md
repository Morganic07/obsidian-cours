PROPOSITION

Suppose $X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n$ are iid with common mean $\mu$ and common standard deviation $\sigma$. $T$ and $\bar{X}$ have the following properties:

| | |
|---|---|
| 1. $E(T) = n\mu$ | 1. $E(\bar{X}) = \mu$ |
| 2. $\text{Var}(T) = n\sigma^2$ and $\text{SD}(T) = \sqrt{n}\sigma$ | 2. $\text{Var}(\bar{X}) = \dfrac{\sigma^2}{n}$ and $\text{SD}(\bar{X}) = \dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$ |
| 3. If the $X_i$s are normally distributed, then $T$ is also normally distributed. | 3. If the $X_i$s are normally distributed, then $\bar{X}$ is also normally distributed. |
