Let $T : V \to W$ be a linear transformation between vector spaces. The ***kernel*** of $T$, also called the ***null space*** of $T$, is the inverse image of the zero vector, $\mathbf{0}$, of $W$,

$\ker(T) = T^{-1}(\mathbf{0}) = \{\mathbf{v} \in V \mid T\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}\}$.

It's sometimes denoted $N(T)$ for null space of $T$.
