We need to prove that either $P(A|B^c) = P(A)$ or $P(B^c|A) = P(B^c)$.

$$P(A|B^c) = \frac{P(A \cap B^c)}{P(B^c)} = \frac{P(A) - P(A \cap B)}{P(B^c)} = \frac{P(A) - P(A)P(B)}{P(B^c)}$$

$$= \frac{P(A)\,(1 - P(B))}{P(B^c)}$$

$$= \frac{P(A)\,P(B^c)}{P(B^c)} = P(A)$$

Therefore, $A$ and $B^c$ are independent.
