**PROPOSITION**

Let $X$ be a continuous rv with pdf $f(x)$, mean $\mu$, and standard deviation $\sigma$. Then the following properties hold.

1. (variance shortcut) $\text{Var}(X) = E(X^2) - \mu^2 = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x^2 \cdot f(x)dx - \left(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x \cdot f(x)dx\right)^2$

2. (Chebyshev's inequality) For any constant $k \geq 1$,

$$P(|X - \mu| \geq k\sigma) \leq \frac{1}{k^2}$$

3. (linearity of expectation) For any functions $h_1(X)$ and $h_2(X)$ and any constants $a_1$, $a_2$, and $b$,

$$E[a_1 h_1(X) + a_2 h_2(X) + b] = a_1 E[h_1(X)] + a_2 E[h_2(X)] + b$$

4. (rescaling) For any constants $a$ and $b$,

$$E(aX + b) = a\mu + b \qquad \text{Var}(aX + b) = a^2\sigma^2 \qquad \sigma_{aX+b} = |a|\sigma$$
