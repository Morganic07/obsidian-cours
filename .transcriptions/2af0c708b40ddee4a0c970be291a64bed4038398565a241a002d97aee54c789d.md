Theorem

If $a \in \mathbb{R}$ and $|x| < 1$, then

$$(1+x)^a = 1 + \sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \frac{a(a-1)\dots(a-n+1)}{n!} x^n = \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} \binom{a}{n} x^n$$

Remarks

1. If $a$ is a non negative integer, then the terms are eventually 0 and the series is finite.
2. The radius of convergence is equal to 1 and the question whether the series converges at the endpoints $\pm 1$ depends on the value of $a$.
