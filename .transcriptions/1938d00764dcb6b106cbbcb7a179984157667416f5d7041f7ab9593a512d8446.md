**Theorem**

Let $f$ be a function from $\mathbb{R}$ to $\mathbb{R}$ and $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be sequence defined by $a_n = f(n)$. We have

$$\lim_{x\to+\infty} f(x) = \ell \Rightarrow \lim_{n\to+\infty} a_n = \ell$$

**Remark**

The limit as $a$ tends to $\pm\infty$ of a rational function is the same as that of the ratio of the terms of higher degree.
