In general, to test:
- whether the **model fits the data** (in next lectures), or
- whether our **hypothesis is a good explanation of the data**,

is to **compare the systematic variation against the unsystematic variation**.

In doing so we compare **how good the model/hypothesis is at explaining the data against how bad it is** (the error):

$$\text{test statistic} = \frac{\text{variance explained by the model}}{\text{variance not explained by the model}} = \frac{\text{effect}}{\text{error}}$$
