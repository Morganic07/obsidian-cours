We are going to use a proof by contradiction. Suppose that the series is convergent with sum $S$. Then $\lim_{n\in\mathbb{N}}(S_{2n} - S_n) = \lim_{n\in\mathbb{N}} S_{2n} - \lim_{n\in\mathbb{N}} S_n = S - S = 0$. However, $S_{2n} - S_n = \sum_{k=n+1}^{2n} \frac{1}{k} \geq n \times \frac{1}{2n} = \frac{1}{2}$. If we take the limit when $n \to +\infty$ in the previous inequality, we obtain $0 \geq \frac{1}{2}$. We obtain a contradiction. This shows that the series is divergent.
