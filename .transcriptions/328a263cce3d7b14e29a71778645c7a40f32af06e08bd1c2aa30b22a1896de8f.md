**Definition**

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ be a series of functions defined on $X \subset \mathbb{R}$ and that converges pointwise on $X$ to the sum $S$. Let $S_n$ be the $n$th partial sum. The $n$th remainder of the series is defined by $R_n = S - S_n = \sum_{k\geq n+1} f_k$. For each $x \in X$, we have $R_n(x) = S(x) - S_n(x) = \sum_{k\geq n+1} f_k(x)$.

**Proposition**

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ be a pointwise convergent series of functions defined on $X \subset \mathbb{R}$ that converges pointwise on $X$. Then the $n$th remainder $R_n$ converges pointwise to the zero function.
