# How to choose clusters to merge

- Choose $(G_i, G_j)$ to merge, 4 approaches are possible:
  - Minimal Jump: the smallest distance is minimal.
  - Maximal Jump: the largest distance is minimal.
  - Average Jump: the distance between the centroids is minimal.
  - Ward Method: minimal loose of $I_w$.
    - $S_{G_i,G_j} = \frac{w_i w_j}{w_i + w_j} d^2(g_i, g_j)$
    - $w_i = \frac{|G_i|}{N}$
