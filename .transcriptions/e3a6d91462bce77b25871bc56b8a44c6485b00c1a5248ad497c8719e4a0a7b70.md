**Lemma**

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ be a series with positive real terms. It converges if, and only if, the sequence $(S_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ of $n$th partial sums is bounded above. That is

$$\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n \text{ converges} \Leftrightarrow \exists M \geq 0,\ \forall n \in \mathbb{N},\ S_n \leq M$$
