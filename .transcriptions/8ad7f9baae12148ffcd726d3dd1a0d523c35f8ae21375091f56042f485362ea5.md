**Definition**

Let $a \in \overline{\mathbb{R}} = \mathbb{R} \cup \{\pm\infty\}$, $f$ and $g$, two functions from $\nu(a)$ to $\mathbb{R}$. Assume that $g$ does not vanish on $\nu(a)$ to $\mathbb{R}$ except eventually at $a$ and that in this case $f(a) = 0$. We say that $f$ is a little $o$ of $g$ near $a$ if

$$\lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = 0$$

In that case, we write $f \underset{a}{=} o(g)$.
