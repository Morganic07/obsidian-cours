Each of these properties **do not** imply uniform convergence of the sequence:

1. The limit is bounded.
2. The limit is continuous.
3. $\lim_{n\to+\infty} \int_a^b f_n(t)\,dt = \int_a^b \lim_{n\to+\infty} f_n(t)\,dt$

Each of these properties **do not** imply uniform convergence of the sequence of the derivatives:

1. The limit is differentiable
2. $(\lim_{n\to+\infty} f_n)' = \lim_{n\to+\infty} f_n'$
