• If $R = 0$ the interval of convergence is $\{0\}$.

• If $R = +\infty$, the interval of convergence is $]-\infty, +\infty[$.

• if $R \in ]0, +\infty[$, when $x = \pm R$, anything can happen. The series might converge at one or both endpoints or might diverge at both endpoints. Thus there are four possibilities for the interval of convergence:

$$[-R, R],\ ]-R, R],\ [-R, R[,\ ]-R, R[$$
