Pearson's Correlation Coefficient is standardized covariance (unitless):

$r = \dfrac{\text{cov}(x, y)}{\sqrt{\text{var } x}\,\sqrt{\text{var } y}}$

**Coefficient of Covariance formulas**

$r = \dfrac{SS_{xy}}{\sqrt{SS_{xx} SS_{yy}}}$

where:

$SS_{xx} = \sum x^2 - \dfrac{(\sum x)^2}{n}$

$SS_{yy} = \sum y^2 - \dfrac{(\sum y)^2}{n}$

$SS_{xy} = \sum xy - \dfrac{(\sum x)(\sum y)}{n}$
