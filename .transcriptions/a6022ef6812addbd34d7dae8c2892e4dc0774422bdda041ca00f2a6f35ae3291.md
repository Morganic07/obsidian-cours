Theorem: Law of total probability

Let $A_1, \dots, A_n$ be mutually exclusive and exhaustive events in a sample space $S$. Then for any other event $B$ in $S$,

$$P(B) = P(B|A_1)P(A_1) + P(B|A_2)P(A_2) + \cdots + P(B|A_n)P(A_n)$$

That is, $P(B) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} P(B|A_i)P(A_i)$.
