- RIP is a **distance-vector** routing protocol.
- RIP shares routing information **within** an autonomous system.
- RIP uses **hop count** as the metric.

**Key Features**

- **Routing updates**: Sent every 30 seconds.
- **Maximum hop count**: 15 (16=unreachable)
- **Protocol versions**:
  - RIPv1: Classful, no subnet info (routing updates without subnetmasks)
  - RIPv2: Classless, supports VLSM (routing updates includes subnetmasks)
- Easy to configure, suitable for small networks.
