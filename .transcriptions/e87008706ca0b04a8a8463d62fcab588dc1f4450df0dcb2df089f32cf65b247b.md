Proposition

Let $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of functions defined on a set $X \subset \mathbb{R}$. If the the series of functions $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ converges uniformly on $X$, then the sequence of functions $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ converges uniformly to the zero function on $X$.
