For some $n \times n$ matrix $A$, a **bilinear form** on $\mathbb{R}^n$ has the form

$f(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}) = \langle\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}\rangle = \mathbf{x}^TA\mathbf{y} = \sum_{i,j} x_ia_{ij}y_j$

where $\mathbf{x} = [x_1 \cdots x_n]^T$, $\mathbf{y} = [y_1 \cdots y_n]^T$.

For example: $f(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}) = \mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{y}$ (dot product → **bilinear form**)

while $f(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}) = \mathbf{x} + \mathbf{y}$ (**linear form**)
