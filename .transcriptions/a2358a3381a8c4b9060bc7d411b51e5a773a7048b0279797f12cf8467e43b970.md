Definition

If $f$ has a power series representation (expansion) at $x = \alpha$, the series $f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(\alpha)}{n!}(x-\alpha)^n$ is called the Taylor series of the function $f$ at $\alpha$.

Definition

If $f$ has a power series representation (expansion) at $x = 0$, the series $f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(0)}{n!}x^n$ is called the Maclaurin series of the function $f$.
