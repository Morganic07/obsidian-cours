**Questions**

Which relations can we have between the sequence $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ and its limit?

1. If the functions $f_n$ are continuous, is the limit continuous?
2. If the functions $f_n$ and $f$ have a derivative, do we have: $\lim_{n\to+\infty} f_n' = f'$ ?
3. If the functions $f_n$ and $f$ are defined on an interval $[a,b]$ and the integrals $\int_a^b f_n(x)\,dx$ and $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$ exist, is it true that $\lim_{n\to+\infty} \int_a^b f_n(x)\,dx = \int_a^b f(x)\,dx$ ?

As we can see, all these questions involve limit switching. For the first question, we notice that pointwise convergence of a sequence of continuous functions does not imply that the limit is continuous.
