**Simple Graph**

An undirected graph $G = (V, E, \gamma)$ is said to be simple if and only if it has neither loops nor parallel edges.

**Underlying Graph**

Let $G = (V, E, \gamma)$ be an undirected graph. The underlying graph of $G$ is the simple undirected graph $\tilde{G} = (V, E')$ such that:

- $E' \subseteq V \times V$
- $\forall (v, v') \in E' \Leftrightarrow v \neq v'$
