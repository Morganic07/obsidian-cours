- Handles with missing values: assume that the value of the attribute $a_i$ is missing in a given example:
  - the training set is partitioned into $n$ groups according to the $n$ possible values of $a_i$,
  - the given example is affected to each one of the $n$ groups with a probability estimated as the frequency of the group..
  - the probability is considered when the gain is computed
  - the probability is propagated in the tree.
- Pruning of the decision tree.
