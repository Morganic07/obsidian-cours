**Definition**

Let $\sum_{n\geq 0} a_n x^n$ and $\sum_{n\geq 0} b_n x^n$ be two power series. Their sum is the series $\sum_{n\geq 0} (a_n + b_n)x^n$

**Proposition**

Let $\sum_{n\geq 0} a_n x^n$ and $\sum_{n\geq 0} b_n x^n$ be two power series with respective radii of convergence $R_a$ and $R_b$ and respective sums $S_a$ and $S_b$. The radius of convergence of the series $\sum_{n\geq 0} (a_n + b_n)x^n$ is denoted by $R_{a+b}$ and its sum by $S_{a+b}$. Then $R_{a+b} \geq \min(R_a, R_b)$ and

$$\forall x \in \mathbb{R}, |x| \leq \min(R_a, R_b) \Rightarrow S_{a+b}(x) = S_a(x) + S_b(x)$$

Moreover, if $R_a \neq R_b$, then $R_{a+b} = \min(R_a, R_b)$.
