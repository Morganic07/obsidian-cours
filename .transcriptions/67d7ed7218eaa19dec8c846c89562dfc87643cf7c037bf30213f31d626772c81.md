Definition (conditional probability): For any two events $A, B$ with $P(B) > 0$, the *conditional probability of $A$ given that $B$ has ocurred*, is denoted by $P(A|B)$ and is defined by

$$P(A|B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}$$

We also say « P(A) is the probability of A *given B* »
