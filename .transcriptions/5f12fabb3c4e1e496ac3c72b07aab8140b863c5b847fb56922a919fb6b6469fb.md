- Similar to one-sample hypothesis testing

- We use the $Z$ statistic *if we can*.

- Use $Z$ **(test/distribution) if $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_2$ are known** or **if samples are large enough**.
  - What is large enough?
    Both samples together must be **at least 32**.
    Some say 60, or more.

- Therefore, we must use **t (test/distribution)** when
  - $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_2$ are **unknown** and
  - the samples are **small** and
  - the populations are **normally distributed**.
