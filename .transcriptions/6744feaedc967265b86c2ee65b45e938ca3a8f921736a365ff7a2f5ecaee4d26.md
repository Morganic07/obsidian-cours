The partial sum of the series $\sum_{n\geq 1} u_n$ is given by

$$S_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} u_k = \sum_{k=1}^{n} (a_k - a_{k-1}) = a_n - a_0$$

This shows that the series $\sum_{n\geq 1} u_n$ converges if, and only if, the sequence $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ converges. Moreover, when the sequence $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is convergent and its limit is $\ell$, then the sum of the series $\sum_{n\geq 1} u_n$ is given by $\ell - a_0$.
