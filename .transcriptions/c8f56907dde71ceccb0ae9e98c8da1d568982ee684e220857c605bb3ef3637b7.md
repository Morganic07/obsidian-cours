Let $A$ be a symmetric matrix, and define $m$ and $M$ as in (2). Then $M$ is the greatest eigenvalue $\lambda_1$ of $A$ and $m$ is the least eigenvalue of $A$. The value of $\mathbf{x}^TA\mathbf{x}$ is $M$ when $\mathbf{x}$ is a unit eigenvector $\mathbf{u}_1$ corresponding to $M$. The value of $\mathbf{x}^TA\mathbf{x}$ is $m$ when $\mathbf{x}$ is a unit eigenvector corresponding to $m$.
