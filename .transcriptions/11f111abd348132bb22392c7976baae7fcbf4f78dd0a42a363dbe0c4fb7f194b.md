$$P(A^c \mid B^c) = \frac{P(A^c \cap B^c)}{P(B^c)} = \frac{P(A^c) - P(A^c \cap B)}{P(B^c)} = \frac{P(A^c) - P(A^c)P(B)}{P(B^c)}$$

$$= \frac{P(A^c)\,(1 - P(B))}{P(B^c)} = \frac{P(A^c)\,\cancel{P(B^c)}}{\cancel{P(B^c)}}$$

$$= P(A^c)$$

$\Rightarrow$ $A^c$ and $B^c$ are independent
