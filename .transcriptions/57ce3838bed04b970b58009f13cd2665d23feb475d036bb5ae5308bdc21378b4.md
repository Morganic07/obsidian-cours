- Model Quality measures, *for each target value*:
- We define 4 measures:
  - Precision for positive and negative: $\frac{TP}{TP+FP}$, $\frac{TN}{TN+FN}$;
  - Recall for positive and negative : $\frac{TP}{TP+FN}$, : $\frac{TN}{TN+FP}$.
  - The (positive) precision measures the ratio of really positive examples among those classified positive by the classifier.
  - The (positive) recall measure the ratio of found positive examples among all positives examples.
