## Confidence Interval Estimate for Mean Value of *y* at *x = x_p*

$$\hat{y} \pm t_{\alpha/2}\, s \sqrt{\frac{1}{n} + \frac{(x_p - \bar{x})^2}{SS_{xx}}}, \qquad df = n - 2$$

## Factors Affecting Interval Width

1. Level of confidence $(1 - \alpha)$
   - Width (of the interval) increases as confidence increases
2. Data dispersion $(s)$
   - Width (of the interval) increases as variation increases
3. Sample size
   - Width (of the interval) decreases as sample size increases
4. Distance of $x_p$ from mean $\bar{x}$
   - Width (of the interval) increases as distance increases
