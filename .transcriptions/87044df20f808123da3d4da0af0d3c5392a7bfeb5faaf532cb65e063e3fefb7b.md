Definition

Let $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of functions defined on a set $X \subset \mathbb{R}$. We say that the series of functions $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ converges uniformly on $X$ if the sequence $(S_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ of the partial $n$th sums converges uniformly on $X$.

Proposition

Let $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of functions defined on a set $X \subset \mathbb{R}$. The the series of functions $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ converges uniformly on $X$ if, and only if, it converges pointwise on $X$ and the sequence of the remainders converges uniformly to the zero function on $X$.
