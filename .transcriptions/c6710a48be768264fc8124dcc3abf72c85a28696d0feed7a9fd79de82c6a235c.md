Types:

- **Deterministic** models:
  - Hypothesize **exact** relationships
  - Suitable when prediction error is negligible
  
    **Example**: force is exactly mass times acceleration: F = m·a
- **Probabilistic** models:
  - Hypothesize **two** components: (i) Deterministic & (ii) Random error
  
    **Example**: sales volume ($y$) is:
    
    (i) 10 times advertising spending ($x$) + random error → $y = 10x + \varepsilon$
    
    (ii) Random error may be due to factors other than advertising
