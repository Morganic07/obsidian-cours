**Statistical tests** help us make **generalizations** about the population from a sample. A statistical test is a formal technique that relies on the **probability distribution**, for reaching the conclusion concerning the reasonableness of the hypothesis.

**Parametric test** is one which **has information about the population parameter**. A t-test based on Student's t-statistic, which is often used in this regard.
**Example**: The hypothesis *"The **mean** of the population is 15"* is a parametric hypothesis.

**Nonparametric test** is one where the researcher **has no idea regarding the population parameter**. The researcher **is not willing to assume that the data come from a Gaussian distribution**.
