This means $\rho$ is a **measure of the degree of *linear* relationship** between $X$ and $Y$.
This **does not** give information about a possible **nonlinear relation** between $X$ and $Y$.

If $\rho = 0$ this **DOES NOT imply** that $X$ and $Y$ are independent, but only that there is **NO LINEAR relationship** between them.
But there might be a strong **nonlinear** relationship.

If $X$ and $Y$ are r.v. with **correlation equal to zero**, we say they are ***uncorrelated***.

**PROPOSITION**
Two rvs $X$ and $Y$ are uncorrelated if, and only if, $E[XY] = \mu_X \cdot \mu_Y$.
