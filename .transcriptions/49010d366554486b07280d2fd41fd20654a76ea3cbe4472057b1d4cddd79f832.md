**Definition**

Let $f$ be a piece-wise continuous function on $\mathbb{R}$. We say that $f$ is periodic with period $2\pi$ or $2\pi$-periodic, if

$$\forall x \in \mathbb{R},\ f(x+2\pi) = f(x)$$

**Remark**

If a piecewise continuous function $f$ is defined on the $[-\pi, \pi[$ or on $[0, 2\pi[$, and outside the interval by $f(x+2\pi) = f(x)$, then $f$ is $2\pi$-periodic.
