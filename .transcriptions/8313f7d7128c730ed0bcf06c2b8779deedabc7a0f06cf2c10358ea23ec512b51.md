**Theorem**

Suppose that $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is a sequence of differentiable functions defined from a bounded interval $I \subset \mathbb{R}$ to $\mathbb{R}$ such that

1. There exists $x_0 \in I$ such that the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} (f_n(x_0))_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is convergent,
2. The series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} (f_n')_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ converges uniformly on $I$.

Then $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} (f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ converges uniformly on $I$ to a differentiable function $S$ and $S' = \left(\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n\right)' = \sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n'$.
