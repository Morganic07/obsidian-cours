The root test is convenient when $n$th powers occur. The proof is similar to the proof of the ratio test.

> **Theorem**
>
> Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ such that there exists $n_0$, with $a_n \neq 0$ for $n \geq n_0$.
>
> 1. If $\lim_{n\to+\infty} \sqrt[n]{|a_n|} = L < 1$, then the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ is absolutely convergent.
> 2. If $\lim_{n\to+\infty} \sqrt[n]{|a_n|} = L > 1$ or if $\lim_{n\to+\infty} \sqrt[n]{|a_n|} = +\infty$, then the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ is divergent.
> 3. If $\lim_{n\to+\infty} \sqrt[n]{|a_n|} = 1$, no conclusion can be drawn about the convergence or the divergence of the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$.
