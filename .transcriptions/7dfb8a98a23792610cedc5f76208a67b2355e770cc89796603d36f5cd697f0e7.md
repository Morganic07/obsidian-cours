The limit derivative theorem can be used to show that the sequence of the derivatives is not uniformly convergent. In order to obtain this result, you have to check that

1. The functions $f_n$ are differentiable.
2. There exists $x_0 \in I$ such that the sequence $(f_n(x_0))_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is convergent.
3. Either the limit of the sequence $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is not differentiable or $(\lim_{n\to+\infty} f_n)' \neq \lim_{n\to+\infty} f_n'$
