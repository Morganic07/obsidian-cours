Figure : Un repère avec les points (1,1), (2,1) et (3,3) et une droite rouge $y = b + mx$. Des pointillés verticaux relient chaque point à la droite, aux points $(1, b+m)$, $(2, b+2m)$ et $(3, b+3m)$.

$y = b + mx$ $\quad(b, m$ : variables$)$

**Goal**: Minimize the function $f(b,m)$ that give the sum the 3 squared vertical distances from the points to the line.

$f(b,m) = [1-(b+m)]^2 + [1-(b+2m)]^2 + [3-(b+3m)]^2$

Describes the sum of the squared distances (vertical dotted lines)

To minimize $f$, we look for critical points:

- $\dfrac{\partial f(b,m)}{\partial b} = f_b(b,m) = 0 \Rightarrow f_b(b,m) = \dots = -10 + 6b + 12 = 0$
- $\dfrac{\partial f(b,m)}{\partial m} = f_m(b,m) = 0 \Rightarrow f_m(b,m) = \dots = -24 + 12b + 28m = 0$

$(b,m) = \left(-\dfrac{1}{3}, 1\right)$

$D = f_{bb} f_{mm} - (f_{bm})^2 = \dots = 24 > 0$ $\quad\rightarrow$ (Theorem from Multivaliable Calculus / Analysis in $\mathbb{R}^n$)

and $f_{bb} = 6 > 0 \Longrightarrow$ local minimum

The graph $f(b,m)$ is a "parabolic bowl", so, the local minimum is also global minimum.

Thus, the least squares line is: $y = x - \dfrac{1}{3}$
