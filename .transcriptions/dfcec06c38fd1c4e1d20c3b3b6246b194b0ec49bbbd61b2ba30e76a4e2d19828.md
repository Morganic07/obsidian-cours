$\forall x \in \mathbb{R},\ \mathrm{e}^x = \displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!}$

$\forall x \in \mathbb{R},\ \sinh x = \displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} \frac{x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}$

$\forall x \in \mathbb{R},\ \cosh x = \displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} \frac{x^{2n}}{(2n)!}$

$\forall x \in \mathbb{R},\ \sin x = \displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} (-1)^n \frac{x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}$
