- The decision tree is an information source that emits the following quantity of information:

$$I(E) = \sum_{i=1}^{NC} (p_i) \log_2 \frac{1}{p_i} \qquad (2)$$

where $p_i$ is the frequency of class $i$ in the training set E and $NC$ is the number of the class values

- The average of information given by $k$ sub-sets obtained from E according to a given test T is : $\sum_{l=1}^{k} W_l I(E_l^T)$ where $W_l$ is the ratio of examples represented by the subset $E_l^T$ .

- The information gain for a test $T$ is defined by: $gain(T) = I(E) - \sum_{l=1}^{k} W_l I(E_l^T)$
