**Proposition**

We have the following properties:

1. A polynomial function is asymptotically equivalent near $\pm\infty$ to its leading term.
2. A polynomial function is asymptotically equivalent near 0 to its term of lowest degree.
3. $e^x - 1 \underset{0}{=} 1$.
4. $\ln(1+x) \underset{0}{=} x$.
5. $(1+x)^\alpha - 1 \underset{0}{=} \alpha x$.
6. $\sin x \underset{0}{=} x$.
7. $1 - \cos x \underset{0}{=} \frac{x^2}{2}$.
8. $\tan x \underset{0}{=} x$.
