When we say that an individual's test score was at the 85th percentile of the population, we mean that 85% of all population scores were below that score and 15% were above. Similarly, the 40<sup>th</sup> percentile is the score that exceeds 40% of all scores and is exceeded by 60% of all scores

$\eta_p$ is that value on the x-axis such that $100p$% of the area under the graph of the pdf $f(x)$ lies to the left of $\eta_p$ and $100(1-p)$% lies to the right.

Example: $\eta_{0.75}$, the 75<sup>th</sup> percentile, is such that the area under the graph of $f(x)$ to the left of $\eta_{0.75}$ is 0.75.

L'aire sous la courbe à gauche de $\eta_p$ (environ 8) est grisée et notée « Shaded area = $p$ ». À droite, la courbe de la fonction de répartition $F(x)$, qui croît de 0 à 1,0. Des pointillés relient $\eta_p$ sur l'axe des x à $p = F(\eta_p)$ sur l'axe des ordonnées.
