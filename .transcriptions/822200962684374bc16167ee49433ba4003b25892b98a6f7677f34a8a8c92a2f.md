Definition

Let $f$ be a bounded function defined on $f$. The supremum norm of $f$, denoted by $\|f\|_\infty$, is defined by

$$\|f\|_\infty = \sup_{x \in X} |f(x)|$$

À vérifier : le texte dit « defined on $f$ », ce qui semble faux (probablement « defined on $X$ »).

The following proposition gives a necessary and sufficient condition for uniform convergence.

Proposition

Let $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of function defined from a subset non-empty subset $X$ of $\mathbb{R}$ to $\mathbb{R}$. The sequence $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ converges uniformly to $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$ on $X$ if, and only if, $\lim_{n\to+\infty} \|f_n - f\|_\infty = 0$.
