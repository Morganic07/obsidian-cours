**PROPOSITION** If $X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n$ form a random sample from a $N(\mu, \sigma)$ distribution, then $(n-1)S^2/\sigma^2 \sim \chi^2_{n-1}$.

Proof: HW

Therefore, the sample variance is a (re-scaled) chi-squared r.v. with DF $n-1$, where $n$ is the size of the sample.
