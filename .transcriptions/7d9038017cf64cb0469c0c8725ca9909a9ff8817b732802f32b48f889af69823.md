**Definition**

1. A sequence $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is bounded above if there exists $M$ such that $\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\ a_n \leq M$.
2. A sequence $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is bounded below if there exists $m$ such that $\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\ m \leq a_n$.
3. If a sequence $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is bounded above and below, then it is said to be a bounded sequence.
