1. If $f \underset{a}{\sim} g$, then either $f$ and $g$ have a limit at $a$ and the limit is the same, or neither $f$ nor $g$ have a limit at $a$.
2. $f \underset{a}{\sim} g \Leftrightarrow f - g \underset{a}{=} o(g)$.
3. $f \underset{a}{=} o(g) \Leftrightarrow f + g \underset{a}{\sim} g$.
4. If $f \underset{a}{\sim} g$, then $f$ and $g$ have the same sign near $a$.
