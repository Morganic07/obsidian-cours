**Example**

1. Since $\mathrm{e}^{-n^2} = o\left(\frac{1}{n^2}\right)$ and the series $\sum_{n\geq 1} \frac{1}{n^2}$ is convergent, we deduce that the series $\sum_{n\geq 0} \mathrm{e}^{-n^2}$ is convergent.
2. Since $\frac{1}{n} = o\left(\frac{\ln n}{\sqrt{n}}\right)$ and the series $\sum_{n\geq 1} \frac{1}{n}$ is divergent, we deduce that the series $\sum_{n\geq 1} \frac{\ln n}{\sqrt{n}}$ is divergent.
