DEFINITION

The **moment generating function** (mgf) of a continuous random variable $X$ is

$$M_X(t) = E(e^{tX}) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{tx} f(x)\,dx.$$

As in the discrete case, the moment generating function exists iff $M_X(t)$ is defined for an interval that includes zero as well as positive and negative values of $t$.
