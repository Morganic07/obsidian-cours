Definition

A power series centered at 0, is a series of functions of the form

$$\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n x^n = a_0 + a_1 x + a_2 x^2 + a_3 x^3 \ldots$$

The constants $a_n$ are called the coefficients of the series.
Let $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$. A power series centered at $\alpha$, is a series of functions of the form

$$\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n (x-\alpha)^n = a_0 + a_1 (x-\alpha) + a_2 (x-\alpha)^2 + a_3 (x-\alpha)^3 \ldots$$
