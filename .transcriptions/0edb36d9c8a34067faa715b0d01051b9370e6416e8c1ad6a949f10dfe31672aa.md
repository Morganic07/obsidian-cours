**Example**

You're a marketing analyst for a toy company. You find $\hat{\beta}_0 = -0.1$, $\hat{\beta}_1 = 0.7$ and $s = 0.6055$ (annotation : sample standard deviation of $\hat{\varepsilon}$). Is the relationship **significant** at the 0.05 level of significance?

| Ad \$ | Sales (Units) |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 1 |
| 3 | 2 |
| 4 | 2 |
| 5 | 4 |

$t = \frac{\hat{\beta}_1}{S_{\hat{\beta}_1}} = \frac{0.70}{0.1914} = 3.657$

- $H_0$ : $\beta_1 = 0$ / $H_a$ : $\beta_1 \neq 0$
- $\alpha = 0.05$, df $= 5 - 2 = 3$
- Critical Value(s) : $-3.182$ et $3.182$

Figure : courbe de la loi de Student, avec les deux zones « Reject $H_0$ » en bleu, de probabilité $.025$ chacune, au-delà de $-3.182$ et de $3.182$.

**Decision :** Reject at $\alpha = 0.05$.

**Conclusion :** There is evidence of a relationship.

$H_0$ : $\beta_1 = 0$ (No Linear Relationship) ; $H_a$ : $\beta_1 \neq 0$ (Linear Relationship)
