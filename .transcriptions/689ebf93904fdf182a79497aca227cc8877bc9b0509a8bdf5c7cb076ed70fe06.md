Definition

Let $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of functions defined on a set $X \subset \mathbb{R}$. We say that the the series of functions $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ converges normally on $X$, if each function $f_n$ is bounded on $X$ and the numerical series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} \|f_n\|_\infty$ is convergent.
