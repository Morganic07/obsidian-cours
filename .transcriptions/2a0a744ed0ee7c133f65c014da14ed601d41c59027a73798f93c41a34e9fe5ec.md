**Proposition : Weierstrass M-test**

Let $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of functions defined on a set $X \subset \mathbb{R}$. Suppose that there exists a convergent numerical series with positive terms $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ satisfying

$$\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\ \|f_n\|_\infty \leq a_n$$

Then the series of functions $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ is normally convergent on $X$.
