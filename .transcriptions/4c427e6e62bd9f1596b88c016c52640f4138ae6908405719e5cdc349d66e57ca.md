Proposition

Let $\sum_{n\geq 0} a_n(x-\alpha)^n$ be a power series of radius of convergence $R$, and such that $a_n \neq 0$ for $n$ large enough. Then

$$\lim_{n\to+\infty} \frac{|a_{n+1}|}{|a_n|} = \ell \in [0,+\infty] \Rightarrow R = \frac{1}{\ell}$$

Here we set $\frac{1}{0} = +\infty$ and $\frac{1}{+\infty} = 0$
