- For testing hypotheses ($n-1$ degrees of freedom):

$$t_{n-1} = \frac{\bar{X} - \mu_0}{s_{\bar{X}}} = \frac{\bar{X} - \mu_0}{\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}}$$

$\mu_{H_0}$ or $\mu_0$ : denotes the claimed **population mean** (for example, a company might be making a claim about this parameter).

- $(1-\alpha)\%$ Confidence Interval Estimator:

$$\bar{X} \pm t_{\alpha} \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$$
