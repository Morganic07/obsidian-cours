Let $G = (V, E, \gamma)$ be an undirected graph. We denote $V = \{v_i\}_{1 \leq i \leq n}$ and $n = \text{card}(V)$.

## Adjacency Matrix

The adjacency matrix of $G$ is a square matrix $M$ of order $n$ such that $M_{i,j}$ is the number of edges between vertices $v_i$ and $v_j$, with $1 \leq i \leq n$ and $1 \leq j \leq n$.
