Sampling Distribution

$\boldsymbol{\beta}_1$ $\hat{\beta}_1$ $S_{\hat{\beta}_1}$

Slope Coefficient Test Statistic

$$t = \frac{\hat{\beta}_1}{S_{\hat{\beta}_1}} = \frac{\hat{\beta}_1}{\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{SS_{xx}}}},$$

$$df = n - 2$$

where

$$SS_{xx} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^2 - \frac{\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i\right)^2}{n}$$
