**NOTE:**
Both of these coefficients <u>cannot capture</u> any other kind of non-linear relationships. Thus, if a scatterplot indicates a relationship that cannot be expressed by a linear or monotonic function, then both of these coefficients must not be used to determine the strength of the relationship between the variables.
