That is,

$$P\left\{\overline{X} - z_{\alpha/2}\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}} < \mu < \overline{X} + z_{\alpha/2}\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}\right\} = 1 - \alpha$$

Hence, a 100(1 − $\alpha$) percent two-sided confidence interval for $\mu$ is

$$\left(\overline{x} - z_{\alpha/2}\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}},\ \overline{x} + z_{\alpha/2}\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}\right)$$

where $\overline{x}$ is the observed sample mean.
