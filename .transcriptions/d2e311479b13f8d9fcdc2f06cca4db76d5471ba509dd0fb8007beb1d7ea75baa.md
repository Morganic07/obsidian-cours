PROPOSITION

1. If $X$ and $Y$ are independent, then $\rho = 0$, but $\rho = 0$ does not imply independence.
2. $\rho = 1$ or $-1$ iff $Y = aX + b$ for some numbers $a$ and $b$ with $a \neq 0$.
