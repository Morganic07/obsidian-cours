Connected Graph

Let $G = (V, E, \gamma)$ be an undirected graph. $G$ is said to be connected if and only if for every pair $(v, v') \in V \times V$, there exists a path with endpoints $v$ and $v'$.

Connected Components

Let $G = (V, E, \gamma)$ be an undirected graph. A connected component of $G$ is a maximal connected subgraph in terms of the inclusion of vertices and edges.

Propositions

1. A graph is connected if and only if it has a single connected component: in which case, the connected component is the graph itself.
2. The connected components of a graph are disjoint: they do not share any vertices or edges.
