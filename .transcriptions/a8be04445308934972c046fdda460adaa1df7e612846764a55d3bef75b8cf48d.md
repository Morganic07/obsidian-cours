Graph Isomorphism

Let $G_1 = (V_1, E_1, \gamma_1)$ and $G_2 = (V_2, E_2, \gamma_2)$ be two undirected graphs. An isomorphism from $G_1$ to $G_2$ is a pair $(\varphi, \psi)$ such that:

- $\varphi$ is a bijection from $V_1$ to $V_2$;
- $\psi$ is a bijection from $E_1$ to $E_2$;
- for all $e \in E_1$, if $(v, v') \in V_1$ are the endpoints of $e$, then $\varphi(v)$ and $\varphi(v')$ are the endpoints of $\psi(e)$.

Graphs Isomorphism

Two undirected graphs $G_1$ and $G_2$ are said to be isomorphic if and only if there exists an isomorphism from $G_1$ to $G_2$.
