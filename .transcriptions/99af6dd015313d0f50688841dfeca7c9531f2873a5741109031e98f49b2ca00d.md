**Theorem**

Let $(a_n)_{\in\mathbb{N}}$, $(b_n)_{\in\mathbb{N}}$ and $(c_n)_{\in\mathbb{N}}$ be sequences of real numbers such that

$$\exists n_0,\ \forall n \geq n_0,\ a_n \leq b_n \leq c_n$$

Then

$$\lim_{n\to+\infty} a_n = \lim_{n\to+\infty} c_n = \ell \Rightarrow \lim_{n\to+\infty} b_n = \ell$$
