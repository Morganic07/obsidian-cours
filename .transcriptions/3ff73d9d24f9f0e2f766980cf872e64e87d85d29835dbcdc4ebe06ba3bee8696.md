We want to find the sum of the series: $\frac{1}{1\cdot 2} - \frac{1}{2\cdot 2^2} + \frac{1}{3\cdot 2^3} - \frac{1}{4\cdot 2^4} + \dots$. We notice that the sum can be written

$$\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} (-1)^{n-1}\frac{1}{n\cdot 2^n} = \sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} (-1)^{n-1}\frac{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^n}{n}$$

We use the table with the most important Maclaurin series and we obtain

$$\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} (-1)^{n-1}\frac{1}{n\cdot 2^n} = \ln\left(1+\frac{1}{2}\right) = \ln\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)$$
