**Definition**

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$, $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} b_n$ be a two series and $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$. The series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} (a_n + \lambda b_n)$ is called a linear combination of $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ and $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} b_n$.

**Theorem**

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$, $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} b_n$ be two convergent series and $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$. The series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} (a_n + \lambda b_n)$ is convergent and we have

$$\sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} (a_n + \lambda b_n) = \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} a_n + \lambda \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} b_n$$
