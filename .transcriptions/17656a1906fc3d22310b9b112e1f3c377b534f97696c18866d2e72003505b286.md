**Definition**

Let $(u_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ and $(v_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be two real sequences such that $v_n$ does not vanish beyond some index. We say that $(u_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is a big $O$ of $(v_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ if $\frac{u_n}{v_n}$ is bounded near $+\infty$. We write

$$\exists K > 0,\ \exists N \in \mathbb{N},\ \forall n \geq N,\ \left|\frac{u_n}{v_n}\right| \leq K$$

or

$$\exists K > 0,\ \exists N \in \mathbb{N},\ \forall n \geq N,\ |u_n| \leq K|v_n|$$

In this case, we write $u_n \underset{+\infty}{=} O(v_n)$.
