3. Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ be a convergent series and $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} b_n$ be a divergent series. Then $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} -a_n$ is also a convergent series. Suppose that $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} (a_n + b_n)$ is convergent. Then, using the previous theorem, we obtain that

$$\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} b_n = \sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} (a_n + b_n) + \sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} -a_n$$

is convergent which is a contradiction. This shows that the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} (a_n + b_n)$ is divergent.
