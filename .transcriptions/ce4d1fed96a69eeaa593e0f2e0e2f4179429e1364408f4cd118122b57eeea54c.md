**Definition**

Let $G = (V, E, \gamma, w)$ be a weighted undirected graph and $T = (V, E', \gamma|_{E'})$ a spanning tree of $G$, with $E' \subseteq E$. The value of $T$, denoted by $w(T)$, is the real number defined by

$$w(T) = \sum_{e \in E'} w(e).$$

**Definition**

Let $G = (V, E, w)$ be a simple, connected, weighted graph. A minimum spanning tree of $G$ is a spanning tree of $G$ whose value is minimal among all spanning trees of $G$.
