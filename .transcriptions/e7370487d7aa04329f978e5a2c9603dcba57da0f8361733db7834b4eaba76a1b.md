Let $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$, $(b_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be two convergent sequences of real numbers and $c$ a constant. We have the following properties:

1. $\lim_{n\to+\infty}(a_n + b_n) = \lim_{n\to+\infty} a_n + \lim_{n\to+\infty} b_n$.
2. $\lim_{n\to+\infty}(a_n - b_n) = \lim_{n\to+\infty} a_n - \lim_{n\to+\infty} b_n$.
3. $\lim_{n\to+\infty} c a_n = c \lim_{n\to+\infty} a_n$.
4. $\lim_{n\to+\infty} c = c$.
5. $\lim_{n\to+\infty}(a_n \times b_n) = \lim_{n\to+\infty} a_n \times \lim_{n\to+\infty} b_n$.
6. If $\lim_{n\to+\infty} b_n \neq 0$, then $\lim_{n\to+\infty} \dfrac{a_n}{b_n} = \dfrac{\lim_{n\to+\infty} a_n}{\lim_{n\to+\infty} b_n}$.
7. If $p > 0$ and $\forall n,\ a_n > 0$, then $\lim_{n\to+\infty}(a_n)^p = \left[\lim_{n\to+\infty} a_n\right]^p$.
