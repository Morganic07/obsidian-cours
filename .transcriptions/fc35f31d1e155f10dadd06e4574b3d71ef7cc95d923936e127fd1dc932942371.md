**convergence of series**

Let $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ be a series. We say that it is convergent if the sequence $(S_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is convergent. In that case, the limit is called the sum of the series and is denoted by

$$S = \lim_{n\to+\infty} S_n = \lim_{n\to+\infty} \sum_{k=0}^{n} a_k = \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} a_n$$

If the sequence $(S_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is divergent, we say that the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ is divergent and there is no sum.
