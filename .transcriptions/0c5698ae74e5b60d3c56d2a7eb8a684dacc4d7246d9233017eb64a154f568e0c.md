DEFINITION

Two random variables $X$ and $Y$ are said to be **independent** if for every pair of $x$ and $y$ values,

$p(x,y) = p_X(x) \cdot p_Y(y)$ when $X$ and $Y$ are discrete

or

$f(x,y) = f_X(x) \cdot f_Y(y)$ when $X$ and $Y$ are continuous

(1)

If Eq. (1) is not satisfied for all $(x, y)$, then $X$ and $Y$ are said to be **dependent**.
