# OUR AMBITION IS TO MOVE TOWARDS A REGENERATIVE MODEL.

We aim to have a net positive impact on ecosystems and society for each product sold.

**BY 2024**

ROADMAP
Melvita's regenerative roadmap with actions to restore and regenerate ecosystems throughout the value chain.

**BY 2025**

PRODUCT
New reusable glass jar for all our facecare ranges to save 4,37tons of glass, equivalent to 7,738 wine bottles

**BY 2030**

SOURCING
Farm 100% of our key ingredients in line with sustainable agriculture principles bases on agroecological, organic, fair trade, sustainable certifications.

PRODUCT
Stop integrating all type of sulfates (authorized by Cosmebio) following our precautionary principle.

FACTORY
Reach 95% energy self-consumption targeted at Lagorce eco-factory through solar panels
