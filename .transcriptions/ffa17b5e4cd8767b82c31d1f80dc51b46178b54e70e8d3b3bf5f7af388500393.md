- In case of classification problem with two class values, a decision tree is an information source that emits the following quantity of information:

$$I(p, n) = \left(\frac{p}{p+n}\right)\log_2\left(\frac{p+n}{p}\right) + \left(\frac{n}{p+n}\right)\log_2\left(\frac{p+n}{n}\right) \quad (3)$$

where $p$ is the number of positive examples and $n$ is the number of negative ones

- The average of information given by sub-trees obtained from the tree of root $T$ is: $\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\frac{p_i+n_i}{p+n}\right)I(p_i, n_i)$ where $p_i, n_i$ are (resp.) the numbers of positive and negative examples in the sub-tree $i$

- The information gain for a test $T$ is defined by:
$gain(T) = I(p, n) - \sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\frac{p_i+n_i}{p+n}\right)I(p_i, n_i)$
