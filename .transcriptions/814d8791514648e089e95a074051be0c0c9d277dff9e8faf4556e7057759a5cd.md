- To calculate the **two-sample t statistic**:

$$t_{n_1+n_2-2} = \frac{\overline{X}_1 - \overline{X}_2}{\sqrt{s^2_{pooled}\left(\frac{1}{n_1} + \frac{1}{n_2}\right)}}$$

"theory" → you need to learn this formula

- We have to calculate **s²$_{pooled}$** first.

  - This ***pooled variance*** is the variance you would get if you combined the two groups into one and calculated the variance.

  - It is a ***weighted average*** of $s_1^2$ and $s_2^2$, the variances of the two groups, weighted by degrees of freedom.
