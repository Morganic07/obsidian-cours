**What is the definition of Spearman's rank-order correlation?**

There are two methods to calculate Spearman's correlation depending on whether:

(1) your data <u>**does not have tied ranks**</u>:

$$\rho = 1 - \frac{6\sum d_i^2}{n(n^2 - 1)}$$

where $d_i$ = difference in paired ranks and $n$ = number of cases.

(2) your data <u>**has tied ranks**</u>:

$$\rho = \frac{\sum_i (x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y})}{\sqrt{\sum_i (x_i - \bar{x})^2 \sum_i (y_i - \bar{y})^2}}$$

where $i$ = paired score.

**Assumptions:**
- Pairs of observations are independent.
- Two variables should be measured on an ordinal, interval or ratio scale.
- It assumes that there is a monotonic relationship between the two variables.
