The **singular values** of $A$ are the square roots of the eigenvalues of $A^TA$, denoted by $\sigma_1, \dots, \sigma_n$, and they are arranged in decreasing order. That is, $\sigma_i = \sqrt{\lambda_i}$ for $1 \le i \le n$. By equation (2), *the singular values of $A$ are the lengths of the vectors $A\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, A\mathbf{v}_n$.*
