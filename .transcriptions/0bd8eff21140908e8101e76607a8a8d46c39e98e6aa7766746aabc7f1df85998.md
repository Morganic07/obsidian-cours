**The Singular Value Decomposition**

Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix with rank $r$. Then there exists an $m \times n$ matrix $\Sigma$ as in (3) for which the diagonal entries in $D$ are the first $r$ singular values of $A$, $\sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge \cdots \ge \sigma_r > 0$, and there exist an $m \times m$ orthogonal matrix $U$ and an $n \times n$ orthogonal matrix $V$ such that

$A = U\Sigma V^T \to A_{m \times n} = U_{m \times m}\Sigma_{m \times n}V^T_{n \times n}$
