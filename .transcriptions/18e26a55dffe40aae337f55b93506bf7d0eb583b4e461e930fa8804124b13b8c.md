Chain

Let $G = (V, E, \gamma)$ be an undirected graph. A chain in $G$ is a finite sequence $C = (v_0, e_1, v_1, \ldots, e_i, v_i, \ldots, e_p, v_p)$ such that:

- $\forall i \in \{0, \ldots, p\}, v_i \in V$
- $\forall i \in \{1, \ldots, p\}, e_i \in E$ and its endpoints are $v_{i-1}$ and $v_i$

The integer $p$ is called the length of $C$ and is denoted by $\ell(C)$. $v_0$ and $v_p$ are called the endpoints of $C$.
