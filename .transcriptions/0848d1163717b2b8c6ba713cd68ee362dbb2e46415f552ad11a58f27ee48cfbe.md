**Proposition**

Let $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence. The telescoping series $\sum_{n\geq 1}(a_n - a_{n-1})$ is convergent if, and only if the sequence $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is convergent. When the sequence $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ converges, if $\lim_{n\to+\infty} a_n = \ell$, then the sum of the telescoping series is $S = \sum_{n=1}^{+\infty}(a_n - a_{n-1}) = \ell - a_0$.
