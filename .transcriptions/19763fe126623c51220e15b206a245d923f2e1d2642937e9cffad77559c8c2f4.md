**Proof.**

Let $(S_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be the sequence of $n$th partial sums.

If the series converges, then the sequence $(S_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ converges and therefore is bounded. Thus it is bounded above.

Conversely suppose that the sequence $(S_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is bounded above. Since the the values $a_n$ are positive, we have $S_{n+1} - S_n = a_{n+1} \geq 0$. This shows that the sequence $(S_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is increasing. The sequence $(S_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is increasing and bounded above. Therefore, it is convergent. Thus the series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} a_n$ is convergent.
