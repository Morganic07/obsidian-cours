This Z test uses the **normal approximation**:

$$Z = \frac{P_{s1} - P_{s2}}{\sqrt{\bar{p}(1-\bar{p})\left(\frac{1}{n_1} + \frac{1}{n_2}\right)}}$$

- $P_{s1} = \frac{X_1}{n_1}$: the sample proportion in population 1
- $P_{s2} = \frac{X_2}{n_2}$: the sample proportion in population 2

Where:
- $X_1$ represents the # of "successes" in sample 1
- $X_2$ represents the # of "successes" in sample 2
