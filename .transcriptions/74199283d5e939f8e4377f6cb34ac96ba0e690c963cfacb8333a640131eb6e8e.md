**The QR Factorization**

If $A$ is an $m \times n$ matrix with linearly independent columns, then $A$ can be factored as $A = QR$, where $Q$ is an $m \times n$ matrix whose columns form an orthonormal basis for Col $A$ and $R$ is an $n \times n$ upper triangular invertible matrix with positive entries on its diagonal.

Schéma : $A = [\mathbf{a}_1 \ \mathbf{a}_2 \ \mathbf{a}_3]$ est égale à $Q = [\mathbf{e}_1 \ \mathbf{e}_2 \ \mathbf{e}_3]$ (orthogonal unit vector) multipliée par $R$ (upper diagonal matrix) :

$R = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{e}_1^T \cdot \mathbf{a}_1 & \mathbf{e}_1^T \cdot \mathbf{a}_2 & \mathbf{e}_1^T \cdot \mathbf{a}_3 \\ 0 & \mathbf{e}_2^T \cdot \mathbf{a}_2 & \mathbf{e}_2^T \cdot \mathbf{a}_3 \\ 0 & 0 & \mathbf{e}_3^T \cdot \mathbf{a}_3 \end{bmatrix}$
