A function $p(x,y)$ can be used as a joint pmf provided that:

*i)* $p(x,y) \geq 0$ for all $x$ and $y$, and

*ii)* $\sum_{x,y} p(x,y) = 1$

If $p(x,y)$ is the pmf of the pair of random variables $(X,Y)$, and $A$ is an event on the sample space $S \times S$, then the probability that $(X,Y) \in A$ is given by

$$P((X,Y) \in A) = \sum_{(x,y)\in A}\sum p(x,y)$$
