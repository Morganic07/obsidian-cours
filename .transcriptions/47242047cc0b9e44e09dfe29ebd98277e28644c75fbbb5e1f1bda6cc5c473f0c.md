For any collection of rv $X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n$ we can consider constants $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n, b = 0$ and the linear combination

$$a_1 X_1 + \cdots + a_n X_n + b$$

This is a new random variable, with its own distribution and properties, like mean and SD.

A very important linear combination is the sample mean $\frac{X_1 + \dots + X_n}{n}$, denoted by $\bar{X}$.

It is obtained by taking $a_1 = a_2 = \cdots = a_n = \frac{1}{n}$ and $b = 0$.

Notice that we are not requiring the $X_i$ to be independent or to have the same probability distribution. All the $X_i$ could have different distributions and therefore different mean values and standard deviations.
