**Definition**

Let $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of real numbers.

1. We say that the sequence is increasing if $\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\ a_n \leq a_{n+1}$.
2. We say that the sequence is decreasing if $\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\ a_n \geq a_{n+1}$.
3. We say that the sequence is monotonic if it either increasing or decreasing.
