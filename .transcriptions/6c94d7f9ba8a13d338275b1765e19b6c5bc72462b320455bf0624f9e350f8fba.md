Definition

Let $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of functions defined from a set $X \subset \mathbb{R}$. The sequence of the partial sums $(S_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is defined by $S_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} f_n$, that is

$$\forall s \in X,\ S_n(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} f_k(x)$$

The series of functions denoted by $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ or $\sum_{n\geq n_0} f_k$ if the series starts at $n_0$ is the sequence of the partial sums.
