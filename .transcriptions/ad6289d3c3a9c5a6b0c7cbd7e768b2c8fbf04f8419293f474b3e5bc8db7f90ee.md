Here you may use the contrapositive of some theorems:

1. The functions $f_n$ are bounded and the limit is not bounded.
2. The functions $f_n$ are continuous and the limit is not continuous.
3. The functions $f_n$ are continuous and $\lim_{n\to+\infty} \int_a^b f_n(t)\,dt \neq \int_a^b \lim_{n\to+\infty} f_n(t)\,dt$
