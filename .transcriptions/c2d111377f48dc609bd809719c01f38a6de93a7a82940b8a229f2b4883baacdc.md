Definition

Let $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of functions defined on a set $X \subset \mathbb{R}$. We say that the series of functions $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n$ is absolutely convergent on $X$ if for every $x \in X$, the numerical series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} f_n(x)$ is absolutely convergent, that is for every $x \in X$, the numerical series $\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}} |f_n(x)|$ is convergent.

It follows from the definition that absolute convergence implies pointwise convergence.
