$\forall x \in ]-1,1[,\ \ln(1+x) = \displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} (-1)^{n-1} \dfrac{x^n}{n}$

$\forall a \in \mathbb{R},\ \forall x \in ]-1,1[,\ (1+x)^a = \displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} \dfrac{a(a-1)\ldots(a-n+1)}{n!} x^n$
