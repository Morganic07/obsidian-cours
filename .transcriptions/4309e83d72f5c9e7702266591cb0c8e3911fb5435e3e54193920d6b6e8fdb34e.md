**The F distribution.** (Snedecor-Fisher's $F$ −distribution). This distribution is used to estimate the **ratio of the variances of two normal distributions**. If we have two random samples, the first $X_1, \dots, X_{n_1}$ from a $N(\mu_1, \sigma_1)$ distribution, while the second $Y_1, \dots, Y_{n_2}$ from a $N(\mu_2, \sigma_2)$ distribution, then we know that their **sample variances** are scaled $\chi^2$ −distributions. That is:

$$S_1^2 = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n_1}(X_i - \bar{X})^2}{n_1 - 1} \sim \frac{\sigma_1^2 \chi^2_{n_1-1}}{n_1 - 1} \quad \text{and} \quad S_2^2 = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n_{12}}(Y_i - \bar{Y})^2}{n_2 - 1} \sim \frac{\sigma_2^2 \chi^2_{n_2-1}}{n_2 - 1}$$

À vérifier : la borne supérieure de la somme de $S_2^2$ est écrite $n_{12}$ dans l'image (probablement $n_2$).

Therefore, for the ratio:

$$\frac{S_1^2}{S_2^2} \sim \frac{\dfrac{\sigma_1^2 \chi^2_{n_1-1}}{n_1 - 1}}{\dfrac{\sigma_2^2 \chi^2_{n_2-1}}{n_2 - 1}} \quad \Rightarrow \quad \frac{S_1^2/\sigma_1^2}{S_2^2/\sigma_2^2} \sim \frac{\chi^2_{n_1-1}/n_1 - 1}{\chi^2_{n_2-1}/n_2 - 1} = F_{v_1, v_2}$$

**Snedecor-Fisher F-distribution**: describes a **random variable which is a scaled ratio of two $\chi^2$ − distributions**, has what is called a with $v_1 = n_1 - 1$ degrees of freedom in the numerator and $v_2 = n_2 - 1$ degrees of freedom in the denominator.
